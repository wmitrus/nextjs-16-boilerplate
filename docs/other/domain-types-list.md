## Best Choice For A Boilerplate Site

**Recommended default: `.org`**

Why `.org` is the best overall fit:

| Factor                                    | Assessment                                                                                                              |
| ----------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Trust and familiarity                     | Strong. Widely recognized and credible.                                                                                 |
| Fit for developer/open-source positioning | Very good. A boilerplate, starter, or framework project fits naturally.                                                 |
| Renewal price                             | Excellent at $9.80/year, which stays close to `.com` while beating many “developer-friendly” options by a large margin. |
| Long-term cost stability                  | Better than `.io`, `.tech`, `.cloud`, `.info`, and other low-first-year domains with expensive renewals.                |

## Short Recommendation Notes

- Choose `.org` if the boilerplate is community-facing, open-source, educational, or developer-first.
- Choose `.com` instead if the project is mainly a commercial product and mainstream brand recognition matters more than the small renewal difference.
- Avoid picking based on the first-year price alone. `.xyz`, `.cloud`, `.info`, `.live`, and `.it.com` look cheap upfront but become much less attractive on renewal.
- Avoid `.io`, `.tech`, and `.ai` unless the branding value clearly justifies the much higher yearly cost.
